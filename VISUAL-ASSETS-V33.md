## Rules

All UI previews are concepts, not evidence of deployed products. Keep captions visible. Replace assets with authorized screenshots or portraits only; remove private data, credentials and customer identifiers. SVG artwork scales without raster upsampling. Project and editorial images include intrinsic dimensions and responsive CSS.

## Acceptance

Static verification: confirm all asset paths, SVG dimensions, JS parsing and internal anchors. Browser verification: check image loading, mobile layout, light/dark mode, cropping and visual quality. Do not claim pixel-perfect or production acceptance before browser QA.
